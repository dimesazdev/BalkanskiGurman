## Sorting Options
You can sort restaurants by:
- **Highest rating**
- **Lowest price**
- **Highest price**

## Filters (Left Sidebar)
- Price per person
- Rating
- Cuisine
- Amenities
- Working hours

## Search
Use the search bar to find a restaurant by name.  
If no results match your criteria, you’ll see: **“No restaurants match”.**
