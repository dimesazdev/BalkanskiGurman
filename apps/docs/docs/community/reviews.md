## Viewing Reviews
- Publicly visible to everyone.
- Automatically translated into the selected language.

## Writing a Review
- You must be logged in.
- Click **“Write a Review”**.
- Your review is **checked by an admin** before appearing on the site.
- Once approved, it **updates the restaurant’s average rating**.

## Review Search & Sorting
- Search by keyword.
- Sort by **Highest rating**, **Lowest rating**, or **Latest**.
